First public release. Ships primitives, form controls, overlays and navigation components (Button,
Input, Select, Combobox, DatePicker, Dialog, Sheet, Toast, Tabs, Accordion, Toolbar, Pagination,
CommandMenu and more), plus the data-dense set: DataGrid, FilterBuilder, MetricCard, KpiGrid and
EmptyState. Also exports the Tailwind preset with light/dark theme tokens, the `cn` helper and hooks.
